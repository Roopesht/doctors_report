# Intent

## Purpose
Let a radiologist turn short typed abbreviations into a complete whole-abdomen ultrasound report with findings and impression.

## Goals
- G1: Every report has consistent findings, correct impressions that match its findings (e.g. severity from measurements, no contradicting impressions)
- G2: The radiologist produces a report by typing only short codes
- G3: The radiologist produces a report by by voice only short codes
- G4: Reports follow the male and female templates, follow specific order
- G5: All organs (lines) must be present in the report. When an organ is not present, provide information to provide information for missing organs (e.g. "not visualized" or "normal")

## Not goals
- Not stated in the documents (to confirm)

## Success
- A report from abbreviations matches the wording of the male sample (doc 127) for a male patient
- A report from abbreviations matches the wording of the female sample (doc 128) for a female patient
- Impressions agree with the findings, including measurement-based severity
